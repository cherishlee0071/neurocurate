# Phase 0B confound analysis

This is exploratory and preserves the frozen Phase 0 definitions. Raw RSA retains global layer alignment; layer-centered RSA subtracts each subject/layer mean over the five windows. Reliability is noisy and is not treated as an exact independent covariate.

The reliability/preferred-position Spearman association over 25 dependent subject-window observations is -0.5831. Reliability/mean-RSA association is 0.3792. These are descriptive audits, not population inference. Late-window low reliability remains a plausible explanation for part of the apparent depth progression.
